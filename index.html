What happens to my text?
The text you enter is processed in this tab. MaskDrop has no upload endpoint, analytics, external fonts, or AI calls. It does not save your text to browser storage. Closing or reloading the tab clears your workspace.
Detected credentials and optional personal details receive numbered labels. Repeated values keep the same label so your snippet remains useful for debugging. Custom words are matched exactly, with case preserved. Detection is heuristic and incomplete; encrypted, encoded, split, or unfamiliar formats may be missed. Use a reviewed local copy for sensitive work.
